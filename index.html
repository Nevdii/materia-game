Nevdi's Materia Game

Just by watching the stream you earn XP. Spend it rolling for Final Fantasy VII materia, equip your best, and fight your way through the bosses — in order.

Overview Read the Guide See the Hiscores

How it works

  1. Watch. Every minute you're in chat while the stream is live, you earn 1 XP. No typing needed.
  2. Roll. Spend XP in chat with !rollmateria <colour> to roll for materia.
  3. Equip. !equip your best materia into your weapon and armour slots. !materia shows your screen, ending with your Equipped Materia Value.
  4. Upgrade. Merge copies of a materia into its next tier with !upgrade, or !salvage spares back into XP.
  5. Fight. Players can open a boss window with 10,000 Channel Points or 500 Bits. When this window opens, !fight <boss>.

Upgrading materia

!upgrade <materia> merges copies of a materia into one copy of its next tier. The upgraded tiers can't be rolled directly - merging is the only way to get them. Most steps need 5 of the tier before it; the last step of a spell line (Fire2 → Fire3 and so on) only needs 2. So reaching Fire3 takes 10 Fire in total.

Salvaging materia

!salvage <materia> breaks down one spare copy of a materia for XP, for clearing out duplicates you don't need. You get 1/3 of its XP value, rounded down. A materia's XP value is what its color costs to roll:

ColorRoll costSalvage returns
Purple10 XP3 XP
Yellow30 XP10 XP
Green40 XP13 XP
Red100 XP33 XP

So !salvage Ifrit (Red) returns 33 XP. An upgrade-only tier (Fire2, HP Max, Mug, ...) is worth as many copies of the tier it's merged from as it took to make it - so Fire2 (5 Fire) returns 66 XP and Fire3 (2 Fire2) returns 133. Only copies that aren't in a loadout slot can be salvaged; it never un-equips anything (!unequip first if you want to break down an equipped one). One copy per command.

Equipping materia

Materia aren't consumed when equipped — same as the actual game, they're just slotted in. Each slot holds one materia, and only one copy of each materia can be equipped at a time: you can wear one Bolt, one Bolt2 and one Bolt3 together, or one Ifrit and one Shiva, but never two Ifrits. Spare copies stay in your inventory for upgrading and salvaging. If you try to equip a duplicate, the bot tells you it's already equipped.

!equip fills the next open slot automatically — you don't pick a slot number, the bot does, in equipment order (weapon first, then bangle). You can equip several at once by separating names with commas:

!equip Fire, Ice, Knights of the Round

Each name is processed left to right, so this fills up to three empty slots in one command. If a name isn't something you currently have a spare copy of, you'll get back:

Error - You don't have that materia to equip.

Equipping multiple items in one command and only one fails still equips the rest — the reply lists each item's outcome. If your loadout is already full, !unequip <slot|materia> frees a slot first, or !unequip all empties every slot at once.

Pets

Chocobo Lure (Purple, Rare) isn't just a stat stick: while it's equipped, every time you receive XP from watching, you get a hidden 1-in-5000 roll at a Baby Chocobo pet. There's no sign of the roll in chat unless it hits, at which point the bot announces <user> feels like they're being followed. Received Baby Chocobo!. You can only own one, so once you have it the rolls stop. It shows up at the end of your !materia screen as Pet: Baby Chocobo - Ultra Rare. (Only passive watch-time XP counts - an admin !give xp doesn't roll.) At the default 1 XP per minute that's about 5000 minutes (~83 hours) of watching with the Lure equipped, on average.

Privacy

The hiscores show your Twitch username, how many bosses you've defeated, the value of your equipped materia, and any pet you own. The bot also stores your Twitch username and ID, your XP, your materia and your fight results so the game can work.

You can leave the leaderboard and this page at any time by typing !hide in chat (!unhide brings you back). To have your data deleted, ask Nevdi in chat or on Twitch.
